# Comment for erdosproblems.com/197 (post in the problem's forum thread)

**The canonical dyadic partition cannot witness a YES answer.**

Let S_A = ⋃_{k even} (2^{k−1}, 2^k] and S_B = ℤ⁺ ∖ S_A — the "doubling
blocks" partition that is the natural first candidate here, since the
completion 2y − x of any within-block pair lands in the other part.

**Theorem.** S_A admits no permutation of order type ω free of monotone
3-term APs. Hence the dyadic partition does not resolve this problem.

*Proof shape.* (1) An exact "chunk" reformulation: an admissible
permutation exists iff a stage function into finite chunks satisfies two
checkable conditions (in these coordinates the DEGS theorem, in its
monotone form, has a four-line proof). (2) An unconditional reduction
(proved directly on positions): if a certain order gadget OG(M) on the
interval (M, 2M] — AP-freeness plus fifteen precedence axioms generated by
the two values 15 and 16 — is infeasible at infinitely many dyadic scales
M = 2^{2t−1}, then S_A is not permutable. (3) A hand proof that already a
three-axiom core of OG(M), C3 = {2M−5 ≺ M+5, 2M−3 ≺ M+6, 2M−10 ≺ M+3},
is inconsistent with AP-freeness for every M ≡ 0 (mod 8) — which covers
all dyadic scales. The mod-8 condition is sharp (C3 is satisfiable at all
other residues), and the residue enters the hand proof at exactly one
point, a class condition on two "flood centres" near 3M/2.

The discovery phase was heavily machine-assisted (SAT solvers), but the
final chain is human-readable; its finitely many schema instances are
machine-audited by three independently written checkers (with mutation
testing of the checkers), plus DRAT certificates for the finite base
cases. A one-command reproduction kit is in the repository:

- Paper: [arXiv link once live]
- Code, certificates, reproduce.sh: https://github.com/Wkasel/erdos197

**This does not resolve #197**, which remains open: any partition
witnessing a YES answer must differ from the dyadic one. Notably, the
theorem is consistent with Geneson's arXiv:2608.12604, whose 3-permutable
witness uses exactly these octave blocks *minus their bottom slivers* —
the precise region where our obstruction lives.
